01 / The problem

A label is a legal document. Nobody reads it like one.

Net quantity, price, maker, date, customer care: each has a clause, a format and a place it must appear. Checking by eye is slow, uneven and impossible to audit. Checking by OCR alone breaks the moment a rupee sign becomes a seven.

03 / How it reads

Two readers. One set of rules.

The AI model is better at small print, glare and Hindi. OCR is better at telling us where on the photo each word sits. We use both, and only the rules engine gives a verdict.

06 / Principles

Honest by design.

The AI reads. It does not judge.

A model transcribes the label. Every pass, fail and review comes from fixed rules you can read, test and audit.

“Not read” is not “missing”.

If a declaration is blurred or the two readers disagree, it goes to review. We never fail a label for something we could not see.

Exemptions are never guessed.

Fast food, scheduled drugs, LPG and the rest are only applied when you choose them yourself.

Limits are printed on the report.

Rule 7 is skipped because a photo has no real-world scale. Rule 8 is an estimate and is never an automatic fail.
